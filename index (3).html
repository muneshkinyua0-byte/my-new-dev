About

Mr. Munene holds a Diploma in Electronics Engineering from Dedan Kimathi University of Technology and a BSc in Analytical Chemistry from the University of Embu. That combination shows up in how he works: comfortable reading a circuit schematic and a reaction mechanism in the same afternoon.

His academic path began on the electronics side — coils, carriers, and the physics of getting a signal from one point to another without losing it to noise. Chemistry came next, and rather than treating the two as separate chapters, he carried the electronics engineer's instinct for systems into the lab: a reaction, like a circuit, is a set of inputs, transformations, and losses you're trying to account for.

That dual grounding eventually pulled him toward a third area — network and information security — which he sees less as a departure and more as a natural extension: wherever there's a signal, a connection, or a system, there's also a question of who else can reach it, and how it fails when someone tries.

"I don't think of myself as an engineer who also does chemistry, or a chemist who also touches networks. It's one way of looking at systems, applied to three different materials — copper, molecules, and packets." — Mr. Munene

CHEMISTRY · BSc RESEARCH 2022 — 2023

Synthesis of L-DOPA from Mucuna pruriens

His BSc research project isolated and synthesised L-DOPA, the direct metabolic precursor to dopamine, from the Mucuna plant. The work involved extraction and purification of the compound from plant material, followed by analytical confirmation of its identity and purity.

The appeal of the project was practical as much as academic: L-DOPA is best known as the frontline treatment for Parkinson's disease, and a plant-derived route offers a lower-cost alternative to fully synthetic production — relevant in contexts where access to the drug is constrained by price.

Beyond its core motor effects, clinical literature on levodopa documents a secondary dopaminergic effect worth noting for context: several studies on Parkinson's patients have recorded increases in libido during treatment, linked to raised dopamine activity in the brain rather than any deliberate use of the drug for that purpose. Levodopa is not a seizure treatment — if anything, dopaminergic drugs in this class are more often flagged in the literature as a factor that can lower the seizure threshold, so that association runs the opposite way to what's sometimes assumed.

RELEVANCE

ELECTRONICS

Data transmission by AM and FM modulation

A study and build project on carrying data over a carrier wave using amplitude modulation (AM) and frequency modulation (FM). The work compared how each scheme performs under real conditions — bandwidth use, resistance to noise, and fidelity of the signal once it reaches the receiver.

AM's simplicity made it a useful baseline; FM's resistance to amplitude noise showed why it became the standard for higher-fidelity broadcast. Building both from first principles gave him a working sense of the trade-offs radio engineers navigate every day.

AM & FM
EVALUATED ON
Bandwidth, noise resistance, fidelity
WIRELESS SECURITY

Bluetooth frequency hopping as a safety feature

An investigation into how Bluetooth's frequency-hopping spread spectrum — rapidly switching carrier frequencies across the 2.4 GHz band, up to around 1,600 times a second — functions as a built-in safety and security measure.

The hopping pattern reduces interference from other devices sharing the same crowded band, and makes a live connection considerably harder to intercept or jam than a fixed-frequency link — a small design choice with an outsized effect on everyday reliability and privacy.

MECHANISM
Frequency-hopping spread spectrum
BENEFIT
Interference reduction, harder interception
CHEMISTRY · WATER QUALITY

E. coli analysis in water

A water-quality investigation testing samples for Escherichia coli — the standard indicator organism used to flag faecal contamination in drinking and environmental water. Rather than testing for every possible pathogen, water analysts test for E. coli because its presence signals that contamination has occurred and that other, harder-to-detect pathogens may be present too.

The work applied standard analytical technique to sample collection, culturing, and detection, then interpreted the results against recognised safety thresholds — the same logic used by public health labs deciding whether a water source is safe to drink or needs treatment.

Escherichia coli
PURPOSE
Detecting faecal contamination in water
OUTCOME
Safety assessment against contamination thresholds
